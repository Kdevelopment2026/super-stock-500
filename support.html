Support

Found a bug, stuck on something or have an idea for an update? Email kay__1@hotmail.com. It helps to include your iPhone model, the iOS version and what happened just before the problem. Replies usually come within two working days (UK time).

Controls

  • Steering: tilt the phone, use the slide pad or the arrow buttons (Settings › Steering). Tilt sensitivity has three levels.
  • Pedals: GAS and BRAKE on screen. Settings › Auto accelerate keeps the throttle down for you.
  • Gears: automatic by default. Choose manual before a race to get shift buttons beside the pedals.
  • Camera: the CAM button cycles through chase far, chase, cockpit, hood and bumper views.
  • Assists: ABS, traction and stability control can be set to Full, Pro or Off.

Game controllers

PlayStation, Xbox and MFi controllers work without setup. Pair the controller in the iPhone's Settings › Bluetooth, then open the game. The menu button pauses, and the race pauses by itself if the controller disconnects.

The Full Grid purchase

  • What it adds: Red Canyon Raceway, Harbor Lights, the Magma #8, Nighthawk #72 and Goldline #99, and the three-round Championship. It is a one-off purchase, not a subscription.
  • Restore it: Settings › Restore purchases, or the Restore button on the Full Grid screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. Family Sharing is supported.
  • Waiting for approval: with Ask to Buy, the unlock appears as soon as the organiser approves it.
  • Refunds: these are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Performance and battery

The game picks a graphics level for your iPhone and steps it down once by itself if the phone gets hot. You can choose Low, Medium or High in Settings › Graphics quality. Low gives the longest battery life on older phones.

Saves and records

Your progress, records and trophies are saved on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them, and they do not move to a new phone. The Full Grid purchase itself is always restorable.

Privacy

The game collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
