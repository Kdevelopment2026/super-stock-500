Privacy policy

Super Stock 500 for iPhone · last updated 27 October 2026

Short version: Super Stock 500 does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. On the App Store its privacy label is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your iPhone

The game saves these on your device so it can remember your progress:

  • your settings (steering, camera, graphics level, sound);
  • career level, medals, best laps and the top five times on each track;
  • championship results and the 12 trophies;
  • whether the Full Grid has been unlocked, so it is available offline.

None of this is sent anywhere. It is removed when you delete the app. The developer cannot see it.

Purchases

The Full Grid unlock is an in-app purchase made through Apple's App Store using Apple's StoreKit. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account details; the developer never receives your name, email address or payment information. The game only asks StoreKit whether the unlock is owned, to show the right tracks and cars, and to restore it on your other devices. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchases.

Network use

The game works offline. The only network traffic is between your iPhone and Apple when you buy or restore the Full Grid. The game contains no third-party code, software development kits, ads or analytics, and it does not use Game Center.

Haptics, motion and controllers

If you choose tilt steering, the game reads your iPhone's motion sensors while you race, to steer the car. Controller input and haptics work the same way. This information is used live on the device and is never stored or shared.

Children

Super Stock 500 is rated 4+. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have rights over personal data held about you. Super Stock 500 holds none, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete. Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your device. If you email for support, your message is used only to reply to you and is deleted when the conversation is finished.
